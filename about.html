The Story

PitchPilot was born out of frustration. As freelancers, we spent countless hours writing proposals that never got replies. We tried templates, AI tools, and copy-paste formulas — but nothing worked.

So we built a tool that actually understands what clients want: a proposal that shows you read their job, understands their problem, and offers a clear solution. No generic "I am excited to apply" nonsense.

Our mission is simple: help freelancers win more clients in less time. Whether you're just starting out or a seasoned pro, PitchPilot gives you an unfair advantage.
